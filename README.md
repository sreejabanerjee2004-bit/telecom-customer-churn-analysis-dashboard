##  Project Overview

Customer churn is one of the biggest challenges in the telecommunications industry. Losing existing customers directly impacts revenue and increases the cost of acquiring new customers.

This project performs an end-to-end analysis of telecom customer data using Python for data cleaning and exploratory data analysis (EDA), followed by the development of an interactive Power BI dashboard. The objective is to identify the major factors influencing customer churn and provide actionable business recommendations to improve customer retention.

---

##  Business Problem

Customer churn is a major challenge in the telecommunications industry. Retaining existing customers is more cost-effective than acquiring new ones, making customer retention a key business priority.

The goal of this project is to analyze customer behavior, identify the primary factors contributing to churn, and provide actionable recommendations that can help improve customer retention and support data-driven business decisions.

---

##  Project Objectives

- Clean and preprocess the telecom customer dataset.

## Key Business Insights

- Customers on **month-to-month contracts** have the highest churn rate.
- Customers using **Fiber Optic Internet** are more likely to churn than DSL users.
- **Electronic Check** is associated with the highest customer churn among payment methods.
- Customers with **longer tenure** are significantly less likely to churn.
- Customers without **Online Security**, **Online Backup**, or **Tech Support** exhibit higher churn.
- Higher monthly charges are associated with increased churn.
- Customers on **one-year** and **two-year contracts** demonstrate stronger retention.

---
## Business Recommendations

- Encourage customers to switch from month-to-month plans to longer-term contracts through discounts or loyalty programs.
- Investigate the reasons behind the high churn among Fiber Optic users and improve service quality where needed.
- Offer bundled services that include Online Security, Device Protection, and Tech Support.
- Introduce personalized retention campaigns for customers with high monthly charges.
- Promote alternative payment methods instead of Electronic Check.
- Identify high-risk customers early using predictive analytics and targeted retention strategies.
